[size=6][b]Extra Computers[/b][/size]

Buy more computers for your store.

[quote][b]MULTIPLAYER: THE HOST AND EVERY GUEST MUST INSTALL THIS MOD.[/b] Everyone in the session needs it, otherwise the computers won't work for them and they will get errors.[/quote]

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]A new [b]Computer[/b] in the furniture shop for $1000. Buy as many as you like.
[*]Each one works like the store's own computer: shop, pricing, hiring, management, everything.
[*]Each computer remembers which apps and tabs you left open on it.
[*]In multiplayer, every player has their own screen, so two players can work on two computers at once.
[*]Move them, box them up and save them like any other furniture.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

Buy the computer in the furniture shop, unbox it and place it anywhere. Only one player can use a given computer at a time.

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

[b]Required on the host AND on every guest.[/b] By default, the computer is hidden in the shop while a player without the mod is in the room, to protect them from errors.

[size=5][b]Important[/b][/size]

Before you remove the mod, box up every placed computer. Otherwise the game may fail to load part of your store.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]ExtraComputers.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
