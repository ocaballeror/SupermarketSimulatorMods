# Pack Up Products

Right click a product on a shelf, fridge or freezer with empty hands, and you get a box of the right size in your hands with that product inside.

## Features

- No more fetching an empty box first. The mod picks the correct box size for the product.
- Keep right clicking to add more products, as normal.
- The box is a normal game box. It's saved with your store and counted in your inventory.
- It's free. The product is moved from the shelf, not copied, so nothing is duplicated or lost.

## How it works

Walk up to a stocked shelf with nothing in your hands and right click a product (the game's Take button).

Your hands must be empty. Ice cream slots and vending machines are left to the game.

## Multiplayer

The host needs the mod. A guest can use it too, but only if the host also has it. Guests without the mod simply don't get the feature.

## Settings

You can change which button does the packing.

## Installation

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy `PackUpProducts.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins/`.
