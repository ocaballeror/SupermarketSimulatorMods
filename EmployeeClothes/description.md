[size=6][b]Employee Clothes[/b][/size]

Choose the color of your employees' clothes.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]A new [b]Employee Clothes[/b] card in the computer's [b]Customization[/b] tab. It costs $1000 to open, and then you can try as many colors as you like.
[*]Pick a color for each employee type: restockers, cashiers, customer helpers, ice cream helpers, bakers, janitors, security guards, couriers and the [b]players[/b] (the clothes of the player characters, visible to everyone with the mod).
[*]A live preview shows the change while you pick.
[*][b]Apply to all[/b] gives every employee type the same color.
[*][b]Reset[/b] brings back the original clothes.
[*]Employees hired later get the color too. Your choice is remembered between sessions.
[*]Only clothes change color. Hair, skin, faces and shoes stay as they are.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

Open the Customization tab at the computer, press Open on the card, pick a color and press Apply.

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

The host needs the mod and is the only one who can change colors. Guests with the mod see the colors. Guests without it see the normal clothes, and nothing breaks.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]EmployeeClothes.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
