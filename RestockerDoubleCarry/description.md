[size=6][b]Restocker Double Carry[/b][/size]

Experienced restockers (level 10 and up) carry two boxes per trip.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Works both for restocking shelves and for taking delivered boxes to storage.
[*]A trip can be two restocks, two boxes for storage, or one of each.
[*]The second box is shown stacked on top of the first.
[*]Fewer trips, so shelves get filled faster.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

The restocker picks up a second box before leaving. It plans the whole trip and deals first with the box that gives the shorter walk overall; the second box follows the emptiest-shelves-first order. When it reaches a shelf with two boxes, it puts one on the floor beside it, restocks from the other, then goes back for the floor box. Other restockers leave that box alone.

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Only the host needs the mod, because restockers run on the host. Guests without it see the two boxes in the same spot, so it looks like one.

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]

Works with MoreEmployees and Restocker Priority.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]RestockerDoubleCarry.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
