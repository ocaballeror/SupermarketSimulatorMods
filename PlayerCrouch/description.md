[size=6][b]Player Crouch[/b][/size]

Adds crouching to Supermarket Simulator. [b]Hold Left Ctrl[/b] to crouch.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Your view drops and you walk at half speed. You can't sprint or jump while crouched.
[*]You fit under low things. When you let go, you only stand up if there is room above you.
[*]Your character model crouches too. Other players with the mod see it.
[*]Optional toggle mode: press once to crouch, again to stand.
[*]Ctrl still works as the game's snap key while placing furniture.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

You need the mod to crouch, as host or guest. [b]To see another player crouch, BOTH players must have the mod installed.[/b] Players without it just see you standing.

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]

You can change the keyboard key, set a gamepad button, or switch to toggle mode.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]PlayerCrouch.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
