# Bulk Shelf Transfer

Move a whole shelf slot's worth of products with one click, instead of holding the mouse button.

## Features

- **Shift + right click** on a shelf, fridge or freezer slot while holding a container: everything in the slot goes into the container.
- **Shift + left click**: everything in the container that fits goes onto the slot.
- Works with product boxes, online-order paper bags and multi-product crates.
- When you pack a paper bag for an order, it only takes as many products as the order still needs, so you can't over-fill it.
- Normal clicks without Shift work exactly as before.

## How it works

The game's normal rules still apply. Wrong product, wrong box size or a full container gives you the usual warning and nothing moves. Products fly with the normal animation, just quickly one after another.

## Multiplayer

Only the player who wants to use it needs the mod. Everyone else sees the products move as usual.

## Settings

You can change the Shift key to another key.

## Installation

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy `BulkShelfTransfer.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins/`.
