[size=6][b]Bulk Shelf Transfer[/b][/size]

Move a whole shelf slot's worth of products with one click, instead of holding the mouse button.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Shift + right click[/b] on a shelf, fridge or freezer slot while holding a container: everything in the slot goes into the container.
[*][b]Shift + left click[/b]: everything in the container that fits goes onto the slot.
[*]Works with product boxes, online-order paper bags and multi-product crates.
[*]When you pack a paper bag for an order, a product the order still needs is taken only up to what the order needs, so you can't over-fill it. Any other product fills the bag like the crate does.
[*]Normal clicks without Shift work exactly as before.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

The game's normal rules still apply. Wrong product, wrong box size or a full container gives you the usual warning and nothing moves. Products fly with the normal animation, just quickly one after another.

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Only the player who wants to use it needs the mod. Everyone else sees the products move as usual.

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]

You can change the Shift key to another key.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]BulkShelfTransfer.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
