[size=6][b]Player Double Carry[/b][/size]

Carry two boxes at once.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Hold a closed box, look at another closed box and left click. Now you carry both.
[*]Drop, throw, rack or trash the top box as usual. The other one then comes up into your hands.
[*]At the wholesale store, hold left click on a product while holding a box to buy a second box on top.
[*]Other players with the mod see both boxes stacked in your hands.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

While you carry two boxes, neither can be opened. Put one down and the other becomes a normal held box. You can't carry a third box, and boxes taken straight from a rack can't be the second one.

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Only the player who wants to carry two boxes needs the mod, as host or guest. Players without it see your two boxes in the same spot.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]PlayerDoubleCarry.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
