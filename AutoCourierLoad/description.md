# Auto Courier Load

Your courier loads the delivery bags by himself. No more handing him each bag and pressing E.

## Features

- When the courier is waiting at his parking space, he picks up the finished online-order bags lying near him.
- He only takes bags that are closed, have the customer's name tag and belong to an order that is still active. Bags you or someone else is holding are left alone.
- Bags with the soonest deadline go in first, up to what his trunk holds.
- He leaves on his own once every active order is loaded, an order is almost out of time, or his trunk is full.

## How it works

Pack your bags, drop them next to the courier, and he does the rest. If he finds nothing, he waits like normal, and you can still hand him bags yourself.

## Multiplayer

Only the host needs the mod. Guests don't need to install anything.

## Settings

You can change how far from his parking space he looks for bags.

## Installation

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy `AutoCourierLoad.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins/`.
