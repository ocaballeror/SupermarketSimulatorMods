## Status

Remaining settings: `Enabled`, `ScanRadius`, `VerboseLogging` (renamed from `Diagnostics`, default off). - **v0.2.0 (review round 1):** timers use `Time.time` (frozen while a solo game is paused, like the game clock); scanning continues while he waits with something loaded; `LeaveWhenFull` defaults to off (the request names only two reasons to leave); the redundant `SetPlayerClaim` call was dropped (`ReceiveBag` removes the player claim and fires `ClaimChanged` itself); a second `NotifyReturned` restarts an existing visit; doc fixes (stow has no tween, rejection path clears the holder).
- **v0.2.3 (idle courier, user request "if the courier is just waiting at his parking spot and a bag is dropped next to him he also picks it up"):** visits used to start only from `NotifyReturned`, so a courier standing at his spot (a long-finished visit, a game or save just loaded, a visit that ran out its scan window) ignored new bags. Added an idle watch (`AutoLoad.WatchIdleCouriers`, every 1 s from `Tick`, which no longer returns early when there are no visits): for each courier in `EmployeeManager.ActiveCouriers` without a visit, state Idle, not dispatched, `IsAvailableForHandOver`, trunk not full and at least one eligible bag near `ParkPosition` (`AnyCandidateNear`, using the same `IsEligible` filter `Collect` now shares), it starts a normal visit. After that everything is the old logic (settle 0.75 s, load, leave rules, scan window). Not run in-game. `EmployeeManager.ActiveCouriers` (list at +0xC8) is added to by `SpawnCourier` and is a master/offline structure (guests clear visits first).
- **v0.2.4 (review round 1):** (F1, major) solo bags never loaded: the game's `OnlineOrderPaperBagContainer.IsCarried` has an offline branch comparing `PlayerObjectHolder.m_CurrentObject` to the bag, and `OnlineOrderInteraction` never calls `SetNullCurrentObject`, so the last bag the player dropped/threw/handed over counted as carried until he picked up another holdable. The mod now uses its own `AutoLoad.IsHeld`: bag has a parent, or online `NetworkOrderPaperBag.HasHolder`, or the local player's `OnlineOrderInteraction.CurrentOrderPaperBag` (`m_CurrentOrderPaperBag` +0xB0, cleared by `RemovePaperBag`, still set in placing mode) is the bag. (F3) a `Courier.GiveBackLastParcel` postfix (only called by a player's take-back, host or guest RPC) puts the bag into `s_takenBack` with the courier's park spot; it isn't loaded again until it has been farther than `ScanRadius` from that spot (checked every tick), so putting it down next to the scooter doesn't reload it. (F4) `KeepScanningSeconds` removed (the idle watch restarts a visit whenever an eligible bag appears, so the 30 s window changed nothing but how long an empty visit lived); an empty visit now looks for `ScanWindow` = 1.5 s after the courier is ready. (F5) errors are logged once per source per minute instead of once ever. Not run in-game.

## Game reference: courier (verified in the dumps, not in-game)

All types are in `SupermarketSimulator.Courier`; alias the class: `using CourierNpc = SupermarketSimulator.Courier.Courier;`.

- **State machine** (`Courier.m_StateMachine`, `ActiveState`): `m_IdleState`, `m_DeliveryState`, `m_ReturnBagState`. Transitions registered in `Awake` (master/offline only; a guest builds no state machine): Idle -> ReturnBag when `MustReturnBag`; Idle -> Delivery when `IsDispatched && HasDeliverableParcel`; Delivery -> ReturnBag/Idle when the delivery sequence is complete; ReturnBag -> Idle when completed.
- **Coming back:** the delivery is a `Sequence` (`CourierDeliveryRoute`, `CourierDeliver`, `CourierRideHome`, ..., last `CourierNotifyReturn`). `Courier.NotifyReturned()` (master) does `SetDispatched(false)`, shows the "courier is back" toast, and `NetworkCourier.Returned_Broadcast(parkBlocked)`. `CourierIdleState.Enter` then dismounts, `RecallScooterToPark`, `DropInvalidParcels`, `SetTrunkOpen(true)`; `OnTick` walks him to `WaitingPosition` (`EmployeeManager.GetCourierIdlePosition`, or the spawn position).
- **Ready to take bags** = state Idle and `IsAvailableForHandOver` (setup present, not `IsRiding`, not `IsDispatched`, scooter at `ParkPosition` or `IsParkBlocked`). `IsWaitingAtStore` is the same idea without the state check. `ParkPosition` = `EmployeeManager.GetCourierParkPosition(EmployeeId)` (a Transform).
- **Trunk limit:** `MaxOrderCount` = `Setup` (CourierSO, `[+0x58]`) clamped to at least 1, 3 when `Setup` is missing; `ParcelCount` = `m_Parcels.Count`; `IsFull` = `ParcelCount >= MaxOrderCount`.
- **Hand-over (player):** `OnlineOrderInteraction.TryGiveBagToCourier(courier)` checks `IsFull`, then bag closed + tag attached + `m_OrderData`, then `Courier.TryResolveHandOverOrder(bag, out order)`; `GiveBagToCourier` drops the bag from the hands (`PlayerObjectHolder.DropObject`, `OnPlacementModeEnded`, `OnHeld`, `NetworkOnlineOrderInteraction.Drop_Broadcast`), `CourierManager.SetPlayerClaim(id, false)`, ends the interaction and: host -> `Courier.ReceiveBag(bag)`; guest -> `TransferOwnership(MasterClient)` of the bag's PhotonView + `NetworkCourier.GiveBag_Master` -> `CourierGiveBag_RPC` (master) -> `ReceiveBag`.
- **`Courier.ReceiveBag(bag)`** (master/offline; returns bool): bag non-null, not a guest, state == Idle, `IsAvailableForHandOver`, `ParcelCount < MaxOrderCount`, `TryResolveHandOverOrder` -> claim (`CourierManager.m_ClaimedOrders[id] = courier`, `ClaimChanged`, `NetworkCourier.Claim_Broadcast(id, true)`) -> `StowParcel(bag, order, animate: true)` (new `CourierParcel`, `OrderPaperBag.OnHeld`, `StowBag` -> `AttachBag` to the scooter's bag mount, `RefreshTrunkVisuals`, `NetworkOrderPaperBag.SetHolder_Broadcast("courier_<EmployeeId>")`, `NetworkCourier.ParcelAdded_Broadcast`). `ReceiveBag` also removes the order from `m_PlayerClaimedOrders` and fires `ClaimChanged` before stowing. Stowing has no tween: the bag is reparented and placed at once. Any failed check: the bag is set active, unparented, `OnDropped`, `MoveBagToDropArea` and `SetInteractable(true)` (offline) or `SetHolder_Broadcast(string.Empty)` (online, clears the holder), return false.
- **`Courier.TryResolveHandOverOrder`** (static): bag non-null; `m_IsOpen == false`; `IsTagAttached`; `m_OrderData != null`; `order = OrderManager.GetOrder(m_OrderData.ID)` non-null; `!order.IsExpired`; not `CourierManager.IsClaimedByCourier(order.ID)`.
- **E on the courier:** `Courier.CustomInteract` (`InteractionType` BOTH-ish when `CanDispatch`): `CanDispatch` (`!IsDispatched && !IsRiding && Setup != null && m_Parcels.Count > 0`) -> master: `RequestDispatch()`; guest: `NetworkCourier.Dispatch_Master` -> `CourierDispatch_RPC` (master only) -> `RequestDispatch()`. `RequestDispatch` (bool): not a guest, state == Idle, `CanDispatch`, `HasDeliverableParcel` -> `SetDispatched(true)`. `SetDispatched` broadcasts through `NetworkCourier.Dispatched_Broadcast`. `ClearStaleDispatch` (Idle `OnTick`) undoes a dispatch that has no deliverable parcel left.
- **Bags:** `OnlineOrderPaperBagContainer.m_PaperBags` (static `List<OrderPaperBag>`, also `PaperBags`) lists every bag. `OnlineOrderPaperBagContainer.IsCarried(bag)`: `transform.parent != null`, or (online) `NetworkOrderPaperBag.HasHolder` (`m_Holder` non-empty), or (offline) the local `PlayerObjectHolder.m_CurrentObject` is the bag (**pitfall:** `OnlineOrderInteraction` never clears `m_CurrentObject` on drop/throw/hand-over, so offline this stays true for the last bag handled; don't use `IsCarried` offline, see `AutoLoad.IsHeld`). `OrderPaperBag`: `m_OrderData` +0x78, `m_IsOpen` +0x80, `IsTagAttached` +0xD0, `NetworkOrderPaperBag` +0xE8. Picking a bag up (`OnlineOrderInteraction.TakePaperBag`) transfers its PhotonView to the taker (so ownership is `Takeover`-style); `OrderPaperBag.OnPlacedOnPlacementArea` and `PlaceBoxToPlacementArea` give it back to the master.
- **Drop area:** `CourierBagDropArea` is where the *courier puts bags back* (rejected hand-over, released parcels: `Courier.MoveBagToDropArea`, `CourierPlaceBag` task, `CourierReturnBagState`). Nothing in the game takes bags from it; it is not an intake.
- **Order deadline:** `OrderListData` +0x3C `ExpirationDay`, +0x40 `ExpirationTime` (game seconds of the day, same axis as `DayCycleManager.CurrentTime`), +0x44 `IsExpired`, +0x58 `HasExpiration`. `OnlineOrderCustomer.CheckExpiration` (master) expires when `CurrentDay > ExpirationDay` or (same day and `CurrentTime >= ExpirationTime`). The countdown on the note (`OrderTimeDisplay.Update`): `(ExpirationTime - CurrentTime)`, plus `OrderManager.m_DayEndTime` (46800, set in the ctor) when `ExpirationDay > CurrentDay`, divided by `DayCycleManager.GameTimeScale` (game seconds per real second), shown as `MM:SS`; yellow under 240 s, red under 120 s (constants at `0x1838CF148`, `0x1838CF100`). `OrderManager.CalculateExpireDate(day, orderTime)` = order time + hours x 3600 from the current `OrderSpawnSettingsSO`, wrapping at 46800.
- **Active orders:** `OrderManager.GeneratedOrderLists` (see SupermarketFixes/CLAUDE.md). `CourierManager.IsClaimedByCourier(id)` = `m_ClaimedOrders.ContainsKey`; `m_PlayerClaimedOrders` is a separate set that `OnAttachTag`/`OnDetachTag`/`GiveBagToCourier` change (`SetPlayerClaim`, fires `ClaimChanged` too).

## How the mod works

- A **visit** for a courier starts from a `Courier.NotifyReturned` postfix (master only) or from the idle watch (v0.2.3; see Status) when a ready, non-full courier without a visit has an eligible bag near his spot. `AutoCourierBehaviour.Update` -> `AutoLoad.Tick` every 0.4 s steps each visit (host/solo only; guests clear their visits).
- Until he is ready the visit only tracks bags near `ParkPosition` (first-seen timers, so a bag already lying there when he arrives is "settled" at once). A candidate bag: active, `!IsHeld` (own check, see v0.2.4), within `ScanRadius` of `ParkPosition`, passes `TryResolveHandOverOrder`, lying free for 0.75 s, not in the courier-returned ignore set, not in the taken-back set. Sorted by real seconds left (no deadline = last), then distance.
- `Load`: online, takes over the bag's PhotonView if not `IsMine` (`TransferOwnership(LocalPlayer)`; the guest hand-over transfers it to the master first, and the bag is `Takeover`); `Courier.ReceiveBag(bag)` (it clears the player claim itself). Before each load the mod checks the same readiness conditions `ReceiveBag` checks, because a failed `ReceiveBag` throws the bag into the drop area.
- After the last load + 1.2 s (a short pause; stowing is instant): `TryLeave` -> reasons in order: an order in `m_Parcels` under `LeaveUnderSeconds` real seconds; no active order left unclaimed (`GeneratedOrderLists` all `IsClaimedByCourier`); trunk full (`Capacity` = the trunk limit). Then `RequestDispatch()`. A refusal retries after 3 s.
- `Courier.GiveBackLastParcel` postfix (a player took the last bag back out of the trunk via `OnlineOrderInteraction.TryTakeBackFromCourier`, or the guest `CourierTakeBack_RPC`) records the bag and the courier's park spot in `s_takenBack`; `ReleaseTakenBack` (every tick) forgets it once the bag is farther than `ScanRadius` from that spot, so the player can carry it out and back in to have it loaded.
- `Courier.MoveBagToDropArea` postfix records the bag's id and position; it is ignored until it moves 0.5 m or is seen carried (stops a load -> reject -> load loop).
- A visit ends when the courier is dispatched / busy, was never ready within 180 s, or nothing was loaded and the scan window (`ScanWindow`, 1.5 s after he is ready) is over. With something loaded he keeps scanning until dispatched or busy. A repeated `NotifyReturned` for the same courier resets the visit. Timers use `Time.time`. A courier already waiting (game start, save load, or after a visit ran out) gets a new visit from the idle watch as soon as an eligible bag lies near his spot; he is not dispatched unless the mod loaded a bag in that visit.
- Config: `[General] Enabled` (live unpatch/re-apply in `Plugin.SetActive`, `AutoLoad.Reset`), `ScanRadius`, `[Diagnostics] VerboseLogging` (default off). `LeaveUnderSeconds` (60) is a constant in `Plugin.cs` (the trunk limit is the only cap and he always leaves when full). Typed-number settings use `ClampedFloat` (no sliders).

## Multiplayer reasoning

- The AI, the claim table, `ReceiveBag` and `RequestDispatch` are master-side; the mod runs only when `!PhotonNetwork.IsConnectedAndSlave`, calls only game methods, and sends no RPC of its own. The game's own broadcasts (`Claim_Broadcast`, `SetHolder_Broadcast`, `ParcelAdded_Broadcast`, `Dispatched_Broadcast`) carry the result to guests, so an unmodded guest sees what it sees when the host hands a bag over.
- Held bags are never touched: `IsHeld` is true for a bag under any transform parent and (online) for a non-empty holder, which `Take_RPC`/`Drop_RPC` set for guests as well. Residual race (not mitigated on purpose): a guest takes a bag locally and the host only learns of it one round trip later (`Take_RPC`), so a bag a guest grabs at the very moment the courier loads it (typically the first scan after he becomes ready, when bags lying there are already settled) can end up in both places. The same race exists in vanilla between two players. A "skip bags with a remote player nearby" filter was rejected: it would block the normal case of a guest dropping a bag by the courier and standing next to it.
- Compatible with Bug #9's `CourierOrdersFix` (SupermarketFixes): `ReceiveBag` fires `ClaimChanged` as always, so the notebook hides the order on the screens that run that fix.

## Open questions for the in-game test

- Is `ParkPosition` where the scooter really stands, and is 4 m the right size? Is the drop area (where he gives bags back) inside that radius? (Harmless thanks to the ignore set.)
- Does `TransferOwnership(LocalPlayer)` work on a bag a guest owns (is the view `Takeover`)? Does the loaded bag stay in the trunk for guests (no jitter from a guest-owned transform view)? A guest-dropped bag is probably already master-owned; if the log shows ownership problems, restrict the mod to `IsMine` bags.
- Does `NotifyReturned` fire before the state is Idle, and how long until `IsAvailableForHandOver`? (`courier is back` then `is waiting at his parking space` lines show the gap.)
- Is `GameTimeScale` the number the note's countdown uses (it should match the MM:SS on the note)?
- A courier with a blocked park spot (`IsParkBlocked`) counts as available; is the park position still the right place to scan then?

## Test (host with the mod; guest with and without)

1. Order two or three online orders. Pack and close the bags, tag each with its customer's name (so the name shows on the bag), and put them on the ground near the courier's parking spot while he is out delivering (or hand him one first and send him off with E). When he returns, watch: each tagged bag goes into the trunk (all in the same moment), then he leaves. Check the guest's screen too: bags in the trunk, then the courier leaving.
2. Leave one order's bag out: with the 60 s threshold (`LeaveUnderSeconds`), he waits until that order's note shows under 1:00 (or the trunk is full), then leaves.
3. Hold a bag in your hands (host) and stand it near the spot: it must not be taken. Same for a guest holding a bag.
4. Put an open bag, or a bag without a tag, near the spot: not taken.
5. Fill his trunk (3 bags at the start): he leaves when full, even if other orders are still active.
6. Idle courier (v0.2.3): with a courier standing at his parking space (not out, trunk not full), wait longer than 30 s after his last visit, then drop a closed, tagged bag of an active order next to him (within `ScanRadius`). About a second later it must go into his trunk; he then leaves by the usual rules (covers every order, under 60 s, or full). Also try it right after loading a save with him waiting. Holding the bag, an open or untagged bag: nothing happens. Log lines: `courier #N is waiting at his parking space (trunk a/b) and a bag lies within R m; watching`, then `loaded the bag ...`.
7. Solo (offline) game: pack one order, carry the bag to the courier, drop it within `ScanRadius` and walk away empty-handed. It must be loaded about a second later (v0.2.4 fixed this; before, the last bag you dropped counted as carried). Repeat with a batch of bags: all of them, including the last dropped, go in; also try throwing one, and placing one on a shelf/placement area outside the radius (not taken).
8. Take-back (v0.2.4): load a bag, then take it back out of the trunk (the take-back interaction on the courier) and put it down next to the scooter: it must stay on the ground. Carry it more than `ScanRadius` away, then put it down near him again: it is loaded again.
9. Guest race: have the guest grab a bag just as the courier becomes ready; report whether the bag ended up in both places.
10. Toggle `Enabled` off in the config manager: he stops loading at once; E and hand-overs work as vanilla.

Send the `[AutoCourier]` lines from the host's `BepInEx/LogOutput.log` (turn on `VerboseLogging` in the cfg first; it's off by default): `courier #N is back ...`, `is waiting at his parking space (trunk a/b)`, `loaded the bag for order #X ... (N s left, D m from his parking space)`, `leaves with N bag(s): <reason>`, `visit over`, and any `[AutoCourier]` warning/error (`refused the bag`, `RequestDispatch refused`, `failed`). From a guest, only report what it sees (bags, courier).
