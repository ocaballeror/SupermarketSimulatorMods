[size=6][b]Clear Tags[/b][/size]

Makes product tags easier to read, and shows what's inside a box at a glance.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Bigger tags on boxes.[/b] The small product tag on every box (and on furniture boxes) is twice as big by default.
[*][b]Hover panel.[/b] Point your crosshair at a tag and a small panel shows the product's icon, brand and name.
[list]
[*]On boxes it also shows how many items are inside (for example 12 / 24) and what a full box costs to buy.
[*]On furniture boxes it shows the furniture's name and price.
[*]It also works on shelf, rack and vending machine tags, and on any part of a box in a storage rack.
[/list]
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

It only changes how things look on your screen. No game data changes.

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Only the player who wants the bigger tags needs the mod. The host and other players can be unmodded.

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]

You can change the tag size, the hover text size, or turn the hover panel off.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]ClearTags.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
