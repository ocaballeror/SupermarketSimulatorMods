## How it works

- **Bug #1** (`RackMoveFix.cs`, `RackDiagnostics.cs`):
  - Racked boxes aren't drawn by their own mesh. `Box.ToggleInstanced(true)` registers a frozen snapshot of their position with `InstancedRenderer`. When a rack is picked up, only the boxes listed in each slot's `m_Boxes` are unregistered.
  - On guests, network updates rebuild rack slots: `RackSlot.Initialize` → `ClearLocalBoxes`. That clears `m_Boxes` but leaves the networked boxes alive and registered, and spawns local copies. Those orphaned boxes become ghosts and duplicates once the rack moves.
  - The fix adopts the networked boxes back into their slot during the rebuild. It also guards the delayed `ToggleInstanced` callbacks that fire for boxes no longer in a slot.
  - Racked boxes stay networked on the host when placed during the session (`ENABLE_DEMATERIALIZE_ON_PLACE` is false); boxes loaded from the save are local copies. A guest who **joins later** gets the networked ones through `NetworkBox.OnPhotonInstantiate`: unparented, kinematic, not in any slot. `ReceiveRackData` spawns a local copy of each as well.
  - `RackMoveFix.Tick` is called from `DiagnosticsBehaviour`. It sweeps every 2 s, guests in a room only, for 60 s after racks first exist, extended by 20 s after each adoption. It adopts each loose, unclaimed networked box that has exactly one local in-slot copy with the same UID + ProductID within 0.3 m, via `Swap(..., origin: null)`. Copies are indexed by (UID, ProductID) once per sweep. It skips carried racks and boxes whose UID + product appears on more than one live networked box. A rejection is logged once per box per window.
  - `Swap` also calls `NetworkBox.DisableNetworkTransformSync(false)`, as vanilla does whenever it racks a box. The rebuild adoption releases claimed (held) boxes instead of pulling them back into the slot.
  - `Release` leaves the box a child of the slot, outside `m_Boxes`. The `RackSlot.Initialize` prefix therefore also captures networked `Box` children of the slot that aren't in `m_Boxes` (unclaimed, not tweening), with `origin = null`, so a later rebuild that lists them swaps them in instead of spawning a copy beside them. `Swap` adds the slot highlight for them. Residual: a guest-side clerk/restocker `PlaceBox` delay coroutine that parents a box before calling `AddBox` could, if a rebuild lands in between, end up with the box listed twice; a later rebuild cleans it up. Not verified whether those coroutines parent early.
  - Join sweep: when a loose box's only same-key rack copy is further than 0.3 m, `Reject` logs the distance (once per box per window, VerboseLogging only). While no racks exist, the sweep probes every 10 s instead of 2 s.
  - Clicking a racked networked box itself (instead of the slot) goes `Box.InstantInteract` → `AskAvailability` → host `AvailabilityCallback_RPC` → `NetworkBox.PickUp_Broadcast` → `PickUP_RPC` (to all) → `Box.PickUp`. That path never calls `RackSlot.RemoveBox`, so the slot keeps the entry and its record.
    - Host fix: a `PickUp_Broadcast` prefix first sends the vanilla `NetworkRack.RemoveLocalRackedBox_Broadcast(slotIndex, uid)`. That is `RpcTarget.All`, so it runs on the host at once and reaches guests before `PickUP_RPC`; unmodded guests clean up too. It's skipped if the UID isn't unique in the slot, because clients remove the first UID match.
    - Fallback on every client: a prefix on both `Box.PickUp` overloads calls `RemoveBox` if the box is still in its parent slot. On a guest, a loose networked box picked up next to its single rack copy gets that copy removed and despawned.
    - `RemoveBox` is purely local (lists, records, `InventoryManager.AddBox`, label, reposition). It removes **every** record with the box's UID, and when no record is left it clears the slot (`RackSlotData.Clear`: records, `BoxID = -1`; `RackManager.RemoveRackSlot`). `RemoveFromSlot` wraps it: if another box in the slot shares the UID, it re-adds the records of the boxes still in `m_Boxes` (same `BoxData` instances) and undoes a wrongful clear (`BoxID`, `RackManager.AddRackSlot`, label count). The host still skips its broadcast in that case.
  - The pickup fallback calls `NetworkBox.DisableNetworkTransformSync(true)` (sync on) for a networked box whose parent is a rack slot, as `TakeBoxFromRack_RPC` does; vanilla never does it on the direct path. Only clients with the mod do it. This was a possible lead for Bug #6 (a dropped box hangs for others); unverified.
  - **E (opt-in, host): `TickHost`**, every 2 s when `DematerializeRackedBoxesOnHost` is on, master in a room. For each networked box in `m_Boxes` of a resting rack (not `IsPlacingMode`, no guest carry request answered in the last 10 s via a `StartRackPlacingMode_Response` prefix, no `m_ColliderEnabler` tween), once it has sat there 10 s, it calls the vanilla `NetworkRack.DematerializeBox_Broadcast(slotIdx, uid)` (max 8 per sweep, one per slot per sweep).
    - Vanilla path (verified in the dumps): `DematerializeBox_RPC` (RpcTarget.All) → `RackSlot.DematerializeNetworkedBox(uid)`: first `m_Boxes` entry with that UID; only if it has a `NetworkBox` and isn't `m_IsBoxOccupied`: `RemoveBox`, `InventoryManager.RemoveBox`, `DetachDataBeforeDespawn`, master `PhotonNetwork.Destroy`, `SpawnLocalRackedBox(data)`. A client whose slot holds a local copy (late joiner, or a guest after cause A) does nothing locally; the master's Destroy removes its loose or orphaned networked copy, and `Box.OnDisable`/`OnDestroy` unregister the instanced snapshot.
    - Skipped (logged once per box with VerboseLogging): not master-owned (`PhotonView.IsMine`; Destroy would fail after the local removal and leave a duplicate), UID not unique in the slot (every client takes the first UID match), and **the only box in the slot** (no other box or other-UID record). For that last one `RemoveBox` clears the slot, `SpawnLocalRackedBox` then calls `IDManager.BoxSO(-1)` → null and returns null: the box is gone on every client. That is a likely reason the game's own caller (`AddBox` `b__2`, `ENABLE_DEMATERIALIZE_ON_PLACE`) is switched off.
    - Busy boxes (inactive, claimed incl. `IsClaimedByNpc`, tweening, not `Racked`) restart their settle timer. Clerk targets on racks are `RackSlotBoxTarget(slot)`, which hold no box reference (also true for RestockerDoubleCarry).
    - Remaining race: a guest's pickup request (`AskAvailability`) for a box that the host destroys before the request arrives is dropped, and the guest's 3 s timeout handles it. The guest doesn't mark the box occupied before the host answers, so its own `DematerializeNetworkedBox` still runs normally.
    - A guest whose records for the slot differ from the host's (only that one record left) would hit the slot-clear on its side; not expected, since guests get the records from the host.
  - All matching uses UID + ProductID, because box UIDs are not unique (see Game reference: racks).
- **Bug #3** (`StuckBoxFix.cs`):
  - Buying calls `PurchasableProduct.Purchase`, which spawns the box and fakes a "take" click on it. On a guest, the take is a request to the host (`NetworkBox.AskAvailability`, 3-second timeout), and `BoxInteraction` is bound to the box *before* the host answers.
  - Open/close act on `BoxInteraction.m_Box`, but drop and throw only act on what `PlayerObjectHolder` really holds. A box that is bound but never reached the hands gives exactly the stuck state.
  - `PlayerObjectHolder.m_CurrentObject` is never cleared on release, so a box only counts as held if it is also still under the hold point (or in placing mode).
  - The watchdog detects that state, or a pickup wait that lasts 6 seconds (the game's own 3 s timeout never ran); with `StuckSeconds` on top, a host answer that never arrives is handled after about 8 s. It ends the interaction the way the game does on a refused pickup and resets the `BoxInteraction` flags that `DropBox` resets. It then retries once per box, but not for racked, inactive or distant boxes. The retry guard (`s_retried`) is cleared once the box is seen held, or after 30 s; there's no cap on recoveries (the old cap of 3 was removed: a recovery needs the player to click the box again, so nothing loops). F9 on a waiting pickup is ignored until the wait exceeds the game's 3 s timeout (the wait is tracked even with `AutoRecover` off). On the host, a repeat request from the player who already holds the box is granted again.
  - Known, accepted: the waiting-branch recovery calls `EndAvailabilityWait`, which also resets `m_AvailabilityTimedOut`. If `PickUP_RPC` arrives later anyway, vanilla's late-grant rebinding is skipped but `Box.PickUp` still holds the box, which is the Bug #2 state that `GhostHeldItemFix` drops. Rare, since it needs the game's own timeout to have never run.
  - Known, accepted: when the **host** itself is stuck, `Recover` only clears its own state (`SetOccupy`, `IsNetworkOccupied`) and sends nothing. The host's buy went through `PickUp_Broadcast`, so guests ran `Box.PickUp(host)` and show the box in the host's hands; if the host's retry is then skipped or fails (retried within 30 s, box over 5 m away, `RetryPickup` off), guests keep seeing it there while it sits on the shelf. It heals when the host picks the box up again. No networked drop: `DropBox_RPC` drops the receiver's *current* object for that player, which may be stale.
  - Known, accepted: a guest's recovery against an unmodded host leaves `IsNetworkOccupied` set on the host, and the grant may have shown the box in the guest's hands to others. Not worse than vanilla (the player is stuck anyway).
- **Bug #2** (`GhostHeldItemFix.cs`):
  - `PlayerObjectHolder.HoldObject` overwrites the held object (and its saved layer, `m_CurrentObjectsLayer`) without releasing the previous one, and every drop only releases the current one. Any earlier item still under the hold point stays there forever. All holdable types go through `HoldObject` (Box, FurnitureBox, PaintBucket, FloorBox, OrderPaperBag, IceCreamBucket), always as a direct child of `m_ObjectHolder`.
  - Suspected trigger (guest only): the host's pickup grant arrives after the guest's 3-second timeout, while the player already holds or uses something else. The game logs this ("PickUP_RPC arrived AFTER timeout") but still calls `Box.PickUp`, which binds `BoxInteraction.m_Box` and holds the late box. Result: (a) the previously held item becomes an orphan (the next drop releases the late box instead), or (b) the late box is current with no interaction active, `BoxInteraction` disabled, nothing waiting or placing.
  - `HoldObject` prefix (local player only): records each item's layer before the hold (kept if a re-hold sees "Ignore Raycast"), and notes which object a hold left under the hold point; the scan's first warning for a visible stray appends it. Not logged from the prefix itself: the ice-cream bucket grab (`IceCreamBucket.GrabThis`) calls `ThrowObjectToBin` (restores the box's layer, doesn't unparent it) and then `HoldObject(bucket)`, so the box is briefly displaced in vanilla too before it's hidden. Records are pruned to the hold point's children every scan.
  - Watchdog (`Tick`, 0.25 s, from `DiagnosticsBehaviour`) over the hold point's direct children, after `DelaySeconds`:
    - orphan boxes: dropped by hand like `DropObject` (unparent, recorded layer or "Interactable", `IPlacingMode.PlacingMode(false, null)`, active colliders solid, physics on unless another client owns it), transform sync re-enabled if owned, `IsNetworkOccupied` cleared if host;
    - other orphans: same, but only if the prefix recorded them and they are still on "Ignore Raycast"; anything else is only logged ("Unknown object");
    - a current box in state (b): `DropObject` plus, online, `NetworkBoxInteraction.DropBox_Broadcast` (needs `BoxInteraction.m_Box` = the box). It turns sync on and clears `IsNetworkOccupied` on the sender; `DropBox_RPC` makes every other client run `DropObject` on this player's current object (the same box, since they ran the same `HoldObject`) and clears `IsNetworkOccupied` there, so an unmodded host frees it too.
    - Orphans can't use the networked drop: `DropBox_RPC` drops the receiver's *current* object. So other clients keep showing an orphan in this player's hands.
    - Furniture boxes have the same host lock (`NetworkFurnitureBox.IsNetworkOccupied` +0x40: `AvailabilityCallback_RPC` rejects while set and sets it on grant). `DropOrphan` clears it for a `FurnitureBox` (via `m_NetworkFurnitureBox`) only on the host/offline. For a guest's orphaned furniture box, nothing frees it (`RegrantToSameHolder` covers `NetworkBox` only), so it stays unpickable until reload; no worse than vanilla. Non-box *current* objects in a no-interaction state are left alone (analysis says that state can only produce a box); revisit only if logs show one.
  - Free-spot check: vanilla only drops a box through placing mode (`OnDrop` → `StartPlacingMode`), whose hologram refuses overlapping spots. `TryDropObject`'s check (`AvailablePosition` = `m_Triggers` empty) is useless outside placing mode, because only `OverlapBoxes` (run from `FixedUpdate` while placing) fills `m_Triggers`. So the mod runs its own `Physics.OverlapBox` per collider (oriented for `BoxCollider`, AABB otherwise, 2 cm margin) on the layers `OverlapBoxes` uses (Default, Interactable, QueryOnly, WallLayer, Ignore Raycast), ignoring triggers, the item, the local player and the hold point. Blocked → retry every scan; F9 tells the player to step back.
  - Skipped: inactive items (the ice-cream bucket box is parked inactive in the hands while the bucket is in use), racked or tweening boxes, the box `BoxInteraction` is waiting for (`IsWaitingAvailability`; its delay restarts afterwards, since the ice-cream/paint box-up flow puts the box in the hands before the pickup handshake), non-box current objects, everything while another interaction owns the hands, and everything during a placement.
  - F9 drops immediately (no delay, also with `AutoRecover` off) but still respects the free-spot check.
- **Bug #5** (`BoxPlaceFix.cs`):
  - Placing needs `m_holdingUse && Box.IsOpen && !m_holdingTake && m_IsBoxOpen && Box.HasProducts` plus the interval (`BoxInteraction.Update`/`CheckForPlacingItem`). `CheckPlaceProductToSlot` never looks at labels: an empty slot accepts any product and `DisplaySlot.AddProduct` relabels it.
  - `m_IsBoxOpen` is BoxInteraction's own flag. It is set by `set_Interactable` (= `IsOpen`), cleared unconditionally by `OnClose`, and set by a `DOVirtual.DelayedCall` after an open click. `OnUse` on an already-open box does nothing but set `m_holdingUse`. So "box open, flag false" blocks placing until close and reopen.
  - Suspected entries: `Box.OpenBox`/`CloseBox` are ignored during the 0.7 s `m_InAnimationTransition` (C pressed right after opening); and online, `PlaceProduct_RPC`/`TakeProduct_RPC` call `Box.OpenBox()` on a closed box (a late host answer after the player closed it).
  - The fix is an `Update` prefix (local player's BoxInteraction only). While Use is held, the box is open and not animating, and the game's own open delay isn't pending (an `OnUse` prefix records open clicks), it sets `m_IsBoxOpen = true`. It also clears `m_holdingTake` if the Take action has been released for 0.5 s, because a release dropped while paused leaves it stuck. Purely local state, so there's no multiplayer impact.
  - **Crate variant** (`CratePlaceFix.cs`, v0.7.2; the 2026-11-15 report was about crates, which the box fix doesn't touch): `CrateInteraction.<OnTakeLoop>` (crate -> shelf, bound to left click) uses `Crate.IsTargetProductExist(slot)` for slots with a product or label, but for an empty slot with no label (`DisplaySlot.ProductID` 0) it needs `m_ScrollCurrentGroupID` (0xD4, the scroll-selected product group) to name a product still in the crate, else it shows an error hint. The id is repaired only by `RefreshScrollHighlight` (from `RefreshActiveHighlight`, on target change and after each placement), which first resets a dead id to -1 and picks the first group only on the next call; `EnableCrate` also picks. Fix: `CrateInteraction.Update` prefix on the local player's crate calls `RefreshScrollHighlight` (up to twice) when the aimed slot is empty and unlabeled and the id points at nothing in `Crate.m_Product`. Cause not confirmed in-game; logs `[CratePlace] Crate selection pointed at nothing ...`.
- **Bug #6** (`FloatingBoxFix.cs`, v0.8.0): better-supported causes found in review 2026-11-16: (a) the game's own wake-up (`SortableBoxManager.GetBoxesInArea`) fills a fixed 100-collider `OverlapBoxNonAlloc` buffer that also counts floor and multi-collider boxes, so in a big order pile only an arbitrary subset is woken ("sometimes", "another pickup fixes it"); (b) offline `Box.PickUp()` never calls the wake-up at all; (c) `NetworkBox.PickUp_Broadcast` transfers ownership to the picker, so a box a guest picked up and put down stays guest-owned and kinematic on the host, and only the guest's machine can wake it. Original suspicion: pile boxes are non-kinematic Rigidbodies that sleep, and PhysX doesn't wake them when their support vanishes (a held box becomes kinematic with trigger colliders in `HoldObject`; destroyed boxes just disappear). The game only handles this for its delivery street: `Box.PickUp(PlayerInstance)` calls `SortableBoxManager.WakeUpAllBoxesInStreet` (master, online only, boxes in the street area) before `HoldObject`. `Box.FrezeeBox` (clerk pickups, racking) makes a box kinematic with zero velocity. Fix (event-driven, because a scene-wide `FindObjectsOfType<Box>` also walks every racked box): postfixes on both `Box.PickUp` overloads queue a check 0.25 s and 1 s later of the boxes found by `Physics.OverlapSphere` (2.5 m) around the picked box; a scene-wide sweep runs only every 5 s as a safety net for other ways a box leaves (clerk, trash, despawn). The pickup-triggered check wakes every simulated sleeping neighbour without the support probe (a box half-overhanging counts as supported by the slab but would fall; a needless wake is harmless); the 5 s sweep uses the probe. Pickup position is read in a prefix (afterwards the box is parented to the hand). For boxes this machine simulates (offline or `PhotonView.IsMine`) that are active, not racked, non-kinematic, asleep and have solid colliders, an `Physics.OverlapBox` slab ([bottom - 3 cm, bottom + 1 cm], 70 % of the width, triggers ignored, own colliders skipped) finds no support, so `Rigidbody.WakeUp()`. Logs `[FloatBox] Woke a sleeping box with nothing under it (...)` (max 30) and, for an unsupported *kinematic* box, `[FloatBox] Box kinematic and nothing under it (not touched)` (max 12): that line would mean the cause is a box stuck kinematic, which this fix leaves alone. No log line at all while the bug shows would mean the box isn't what the probe thinks (e.g. it has a support collider such as an invisible trigger-less volume).
- **Bug #7 + Bug #8** (`JanitorCleanAlignFix.cs`, v0.10.0). Goal (user, 2026-11-16): the mop is aimed at the stain centre, and she stands where her body fits (overlapping other characters is fine).
  - Vanilla: `MoveTargetState.Enter` sends the janitor to the stain's transform position (the centre of its 1 m quad). `Janitor.RotateAndCheck` calls `Janitor.Clean` once she is within `stoppingDistance` (0.1, 3D, including the −0.04 base offset) of `agent.destination`, so she stands on the stain centre. `DirtCleaningState.Enter` turns on the mop, the rig and the animator bool `Dirt`, but never turns or moves her. The mop is a child of the character root, about 0.15 m right and 0.55 m forward (prefab), so it works the floor about 0.57 m from the stain, in whatever direction she arrived from, and next to a wall her body and mop go into it.
  - Fix, host/offline only (the AI runs only there):
    - `MoveTargetState.Enter` postfix (`cleaningType == Dirt`, `sType == MoveTarget`): `FindStandPoint` scores candidates on rings around the stain: 24 directions (her side first), distances = mop reach, reach ±0.1 … ±0.5, then reach +0.7, +0.9 … up to 1.6 m (min 0.25 m). A candidate must be within 0.1 m of the wanted spot after `NavMesh.SamplePosition` (0.3 m radius) and have no `NavMesh.Raycast` hit from the stain's NavMesh point (falls back to `Physics.Linecast` at 0.3 m if the icall throws; the game never calls it). Body fit: `Physics.CheckCapsule` 0.25–1.6 m high, radius 0.20 ("room") or 0.13 (her collider, "tight"), triggers ignored, layers 0 Default, 6 Interactable, 10 Door, 14 Buildings, 15 Obstacle, 18 WallLayer, 20 RackLayer, 25 Glass, 29 TrashLayer (Player 3 and Customer 28 left out on purpose; Ignore Raycast 2 left out because floor tiles and the entrance doors are on it). From the prefab/scene data: trash bins (`Trash` furniture prefab, the scene's `Trash Bin`) have their only solid collider on TrashLayer; Glass holds the `DustForGlass` panes (vertical BoxColliders about 1.7 m up on the store windows and showcases). Mop path: a 0.05 m capsule from her hands (0.3 m ahead, 0.95 m up) to where the head lands (reach ahead, 0.1 m up).
    - Cost = 2 × |dist − reach| (+1 beyond ±0.5 m, where the mop leaves the stain) + 0.5 tight fit + 0.6 blocked mop path + up to 0.3 for walking round. Rings are visited in increasing base cost and the search stops once no ring can win; worst case about 290 candidates (about 12 rings × 24, roughly 1,500 physics/NavMesh queries) once per stain.
    - Fallbacks: no fitting spot within reach ±0.5 → the cheapest fitting spot further out (mop aimed but short; logged "no fitting spot nearer"). ## Game reference: racks

Verified in the dumps; the behaviour on join was confirmed by in-game logs.

- `RackSlot` keeps `m_Boxes` (+0x38, live Box objects) and `m_Data.RackedBoxDatas` (the records that are saved and sent to guests). Racked boxes are drawn through `InstancedRenderer` snapshots (`Box.ToggleInstanced`), not their own mesh.
- **Box UIDs are not unique.** `IDManager.GenerateBoxUniqueID` is a per-session counter (`BoxUniqueIdCount`, not saved; returns -2 on guests), and guests keep the host's UIDs. Match boxes on UID + ProductID (+ position), never UID alone.
- A box placed on a rack during the session stays **networked** (`RackSlot.ENABLE_DEMATERIALIZE_ON_PLACE` is false). Boxes loaded from the save are **local** copies (`SpawnLocalRackedBox` / `BoxGenerator.SpawnBoxInRack`). Taking from a slot (`RackSlot.TakeAndMaterializeBox`, host) either broadcasts `RemoveLocalRackedBox` and `MaterializeRackedBox`s a new networked box, or for a networked box just unracks it. `RemoveLocalRackedBox_RPC` → `RemoveLocalRackedBoxByUID` → `RemoveBox` runs on every client.
- `RackSlot.RemoveBox(box)` is the full, purely local cleanup (m_Boxes, records, instancing, highlight, `InventoryManager.AddBox`, label, `RePositionBoxes`). Only TakeBoxFromRack, RemoveLocalRackedBoxByUID, DematerializeNetworkedBox and clerk pickups call it. `Box.PickUp` (the player's box path) does **not**. Its record semantics are under Bug #1 above.
- A guest's copy of a networked box created by `NetworkBox.OnPhotonInstantiate` is unparented and made kinematic; nothing re-racks it.
- Dematerialize (networked racked box → local racked box, host-driven, handled by unmodded clients) and its single-box hazard are described under Bug #1, "E (opt-in, host)". Its only game caller is compiled out.
- Destroying or disabling a racked box cleans up its instanced snapshot (`Box.OnDisable` → `SubRenderer.Unregister`, `Box.OnDestroy` → `RemoveInstance`). Only a box that stays alive while outside `m_Boxes` leaves a ghost.
- A player placing a box on a rack (`NetworkBoxInteraction.PlaceBoxToRack_RPC`) transfers the box's PhotonView back to the master client (asynchronously). Clerk rack targets are `RackSlotBoxTarget(slot)` and hold no box reference.
- **Rack space.** A slot's capacity depends on the box size: `RackSlot.Full` = `Data.BoxCount > 0 && BoxCount >= IDManager.BoxSO(Data.BoxID).GridLayout.boxCount` (1, 2, 4... per size; a box's own size is `Box.BoxSO`). A labelled slot with no boxes is never full. `RackSlotData.ProductID` (+0x18) is the label, -1 = unlabelled.
- **Where a restocker racks a box:** `SearchHandler.HasSpaceInRackSlotForProduct(pid)` → `RackManager.GetRackSlotThatHasSpaceFor(pid, ManagementData.UseUnlabeledRacks)`: over `m_Racks` → `Rack.RackSlots`, the first slot labelled `pid` that isn't `Full`, else (only with UseUnlabeledRacks) the first slot with label -1 and `!HasBox`. `IsMergeAvailableWithBox` (topping up a rack box of the same product) is tried first for a carried box. No other rack rules (frozen etc.) are checked there. Used by RestockerDoubleCarry (`StorageFits` replays it for two boxes).

## Game reference: online orders and the courier

Verified in the dumps; not observed in-game.

- The pending-orders list is per client: `OrderManager.GeneratedOrderLists` (synced; orders leave it only through `RemoveOrderOfId`, reached by `OrderCompleted` / `OrderExpired` / `CancelOrder` and the `RemoveOrder_Broadcast` / `RemoveOrder_RPC` pair) plus the local `OrderNotebookContainer` (one `OrderNotebook` per order; `CurrentInstance` = `m_NotebookInstances[0]`, else the empty notebook).
- The badge number is `GetUnreadOrderCount`: orders with `OrderListData.IsRead == false`, called from `CanShowUI`. `IsRead` is **not** networked.

Removing the order itself would release the claim (`CourierManager.OnOrderRemoved`), and the courier needs `OrderManager.GetOrder` for hand-over, take-back and payment.

## Game reference: janitor (cleaning lady)

Verified in the dumps and prefab data (resources.assets, GameObject "Janitor"); not observed in-game.

- `__Project__.Scripts.Janitor.Janitor` (interop namespace and class share the name; alias it with `using JanitorNpc = __Project__.Scripts.Janitor.Janitor;`). It is a state machine: `_stateMachine` (+0xC8) with states `IdleState`, `WaitingState`, `MoveTargetState`, `GarbageCleaningState`, `DirtCleaningState`, `DustCleaningState`, `TrashBagState`, `TrashBinState`. `SetXState()` sets `sType` (+0x110, `StateType`: Idle 0, Waiting 1, MoveTarget 2, GarbageCleaning 3, DirtCleaning 4, DustCleaning 5, TrashBag 6, TrashBin 7) **before** `Exit`/`Enter`.
- AI runs on the master only: `Janitor.Update` returns on `IsConnectedAndSlave`, and the guest disables the NavMeshAgent (`NetworkClientHandle`). Guests get the transform through `PhotonTransformView` (position and rotation) and the animator through `NetworkedAnimation`. RPCs (`NetworkJanitor`): `CleanDirt`, `FinishMopCleaning`, `CleanDust`, `FinishDustCleaning`, `SwitchThrashBag`, `Boost`.
- Targets: `JanitorManager.SelectCleaningObject(janitor)` sets `cleaningType` (+0xC0: Idle 0, Garbage 1, Dirt 2, Dust 3) and returns the object, which IdleState/WaitingState/TrashBinState store in `TargetObject` (+0xB8) before `SetMoveTargetState`. Garbage is preferred, then dirt (stains), then dust. The selected object is removed from the manager's list, so one janitor per stain.
- Movement: `MoveTargetState.Enter` → `SetDestination(TargetObject.position)`. `Update` → `RotateAndCheck(Clean)`: when the 3D distance from the transform to `agent.destination` is at most `stoppingDistance`, it invokes the action; otherwise it sets `updateRotation = false` and slerps the rotation towards `desiredVelocity` at `turningSpeed` (+0x58). `Clean()` switches on `cleaningType`. Prefab agent: radius 0.05, stoppingDistance 0.1, angularSpeed 0, speed 2, baseOffset −0.04; body CapsuleCollider is a trigger (r 0.13).
- Stain (`Dirt`, layer "Dirt" 24): a 1 m floor quad centred on its transform, BoxCollider 0.7 × 0.7. `DirtCleaningState.Enter` (master): `CleanDirt_Broadcast`, `m_Mop` active, `m_Rig.weight = 1`, animator `Dirt` = true, `CleaningForJanitorCoroutine` (calls `Dirt.AddProgress(dt / cleaningDuration)` each frame until progress reaches 1, then turns everything off, clears `TargetObject`, grants XP, `FinishMopCleaning_Broadcast`, and invokes the end action). Progress doesn't depend on her distance from the stain.
- Dust: `DustCleaningState.Enter` DORotates her to the dust's rotation before cleaning; dirt cleaning has no such step.
- Mop: `SM_Mop` is a child of the character's inner "Janitor" node (net scale about 1), not of a hand bone, at about (0.15, 0, 0.55) from her feet. `SM_Mop_1` is the head (a 0.6 m wide mesh at its origin), `SM_Mop_2` is the handle, and `Right_hand_grip`/`Left_hand_grip` plus `RigLayer` IK targets bring the hands to it.
- **Bug #8 (she walks into walls and shelves to reach a stain)** has the same cause as Bug #7: vanilla always walks her to the stain centre itself with a 0.05 m agent radius, so for a stain next to a wall or shelf she stands where her body (and the mop 0.55 m ahead) overlaps the obstacle. The NavMesh bake radius is unknown. Since v0.10.0 `JanitorCleanAlignFix` covers where she stands (see Bug #7 + Bug #8 above); her walking path is unchanged.
