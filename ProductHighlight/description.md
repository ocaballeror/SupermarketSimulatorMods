[size=6][b]Product Highlight[/b][/size]

Shows where the product in your hands belongs, with the game's green outline.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Holding a box:[/b] every shelf, fridge and freezer labelled for that product is outlined, even when empty.
[*][b]Holding an online-order bag:[/b] the product groups on shelves that the order still needs are outlined. Only products that are really there are marked.
[*]Outlines update live when you relabel, stock or empty a shelf, and disappear when you put the item down.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Only the player who wants the outlines needs the mod. It only draws on your own screen.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]ProductHighlight.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
