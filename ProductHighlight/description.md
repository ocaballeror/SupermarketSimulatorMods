# Product Highlight

Shows where the product in your hands belongs, with the game's green outline.

## Features

- **Holding a box:** every shelf, fridge and freezer labelled for that product is outlined, even when empty.
- **Holding an online-order bag:** the product groups on shelves that the order still needs are outlined. Only products that are really there are marked.
- Outlines update live when you relabel, stock or empty a shelf, and disappear when you put the item down.

## Multiplayer

Only the player who wants the outlines needs the mod. It only draws on your own screen.

## Installation

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy `ProductHighlight.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins/`.
