[size=6][b]Furniture Move Cancel[/b][/size]

Picked up a shelf or fridge by mistake? Press [b]Escape[/b] (gamepad: [b]B[/b]) and it goes straight back where it was.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Puts the furniture back in the same position and rotation.
[*]Works for shelves, fridges, freezers, racks, checkouts and other furniture.
[*]Shows the key in the hints list on the side of the screen while you move furniture.
[/list]

[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

It only works if the original spot is still free. Otherwise nothing happens and you keep holding the furniture. Newly unpacked furniture has no previous spot, so it can't be put back.

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Only the player who wants to use it needs the mod. Other players see the furniture placed as normal.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]FurnitureMoveCancel.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
