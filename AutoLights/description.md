[size=6][b]Auto Lights[/b][/size]

The shop lights switch on by themselves at 7 pm, as if you had flipped the light switch.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]At 19:00 on the in-game clock the store lights (inside and outdoor lamps) and the storage room lights are switched on, if they are off.
[*]It uses the game's own light switch: you hear the switch sound, and in multiplayer everyone sees the lights on.
[*]Once per day only. If you turn the lights off again, they stay off until the next evening.
[*]Loading a save that is already past 7 pm, or joining in the evening, changes nothing.
[*]Nothing happens if you have no light switch or no lamps placed.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]

Only the host needs the mod. Guests don't need it.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP). Copy [i]AutoLights.dll[/i] into [i]BepInEx/plugins/[/i].
